[BRAND TONE & IDENTITY RULES]
1. Tone must be professional, objective, and realistic. Avoid hype or buzzwords like "world's best", "guaranteed #1", "100% bug-free".
2. Approved Terminology:
   - Use "AI-powered compliance engine" instead of "magic AI solution".
   - Use "Enterprise client" instead of "Customer".
3. Claims Policy:
   - Every metric or factual performance statement (e.g. "% accuracy", "delivery in X hours") must provide verifiable empirical proof.
   - Any claim promising absolute zero risk or absolute perfection is strictly prohibited.
4. Data Privacy (Clause 1.1):
   - Never publish personal identifiers (PAN, Aadhaar, phone numbers, personal emails) or credentials/API keys.
5. Delivery Commitments (POL-01):
   - Delivery timelines must not exceed 90 calendar days per phase.
